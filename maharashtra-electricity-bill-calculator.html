Step-by-Step Maharashtra Electricity Bill Calculation

Suppose a household in Pune, Nagpur, Nashik, or Thane consumes 250 units with a 1 kW sanctioned load:

  • First 100 units @ ₹4.41 = ₹441.00
  • Remaining 150 units (101 to 250) @ ₹9.64 = ₹1,446.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,887.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹128 = ₹128.00
  • Electricity Duty (16%): (₹1,887 + ₹128) × 16% = ₹322.40
  • Fuel Adjustment & Wheeling Charges: ₹45.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹2,382.40 (Approx ₹2,382)

Electricity Providers in Maharashtra

Depending on your city, electricity is supplied by:

  • MSEDCL (Mahavitaran): Serves all districts of Maharashtra outside Mumbai city.
  • Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited (AEML): Serves suburban Mumbai.
  • Tata Power: Operates in Mumbai suburbs and parts of South Mumbai.
  • BEST Undertaking: Serves the island city of Mumbai.

Maharashtra Electricity Bill FAQs

Got questions? Find clear answers below.

Maharashtra has one of the highest state electricity duty rates in India (16% for residential consumers), combined with telescopic slab rates that jump to ₹9.64 and ₹13.61 above 100 units. Installing rooftop solar can cut your bill by 70-80%.

You can check and view your official Mahavitaran electricity bill on the Mahadiscom web portal (wss.mahadiscom.in) or the Mahavitaran mobile app by entering your 12-digit Consumer Number and BU code.

Wheeling charge is the cost of operating and maintaining the transmission and distribution network to transport electricity from the substation to your meter.
