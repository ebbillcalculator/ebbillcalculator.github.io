How is Telangana Electricity Bill Calculated?

Telangana State Electricity Regulatory Commission (TSERC) approves domestic Category LT-I tariffs. For example, if a home in Hyderabad, Warangal, Nizamabad, or Karimnagar consumes 250 units with a 1 kW load (without Gruha Jyothi scheme):

  • First 50 units @ ₹3.40 = ₹170.00
  • Next 50 units (51 to 100) @ ₹4.80 = ₹240.00
  • Next 100 units (101 to 200) @ ₹6.50 = ₹650.00
  • Remaining 50 units (201 to 250) @ ₹7.50 = ₹375.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,435.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹100 = ₹100.00
  • Electricity Duty (6%): (₹1,435 + ₹100) × 6% = ₹92.10
  • Customer Charges & Surcharges: ₹40.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,667.10 (Approx ₹1,667)

Telangana Gruha Jyothi Free Electricity Scheme

Under the Government of Telangana's flagship Gruha Jyothi Scheme:

  • Eligible domestic white-ration card holding households receive up to 200 units of free power each month.
  • Eligible households with monthly consumption at or below 200 units receive a Zero Electricity Bill.
  • If monthly consumption exceeds 200 units, the consumer must pay the bill for the total consumed units under standard TSERC tariffs.
  • Application must be submitted via the Praja Palana program linking food security card, Aadhaar, and your USCNO.

Power Distribution Companies in Telangana

Retail electricity supply across the state is operated by two state DISCOMs:

  • TSSPDCL: Southern Power Distribution Company of Telangana Ltd (Hyderabad, Rangareddy, Medchal, Mahabubnagar, Nalgonda, Medak)
  • TSNPDCL: Northern Power Distribution Company of Telangana Ltd (Warangal, Karimnagar, Nizamabad, Khammam, Adilabad)

Telangana Electricity Bill FAQs

Frequently asked questions regarding power bills in Telangana.

You can pay your TSSPDCL bill via the TSSPDCL official website (tssouthernpower.com), the TSSPDCL mobile app, MeeSeva, or payment apps like PhonePe and Paytm using your Unique Service Number (USCNO).

USCNO (Unique Service Connection Number) is a 9-digit or 10-digit number assigned to every power connection in Telangana, required for all online payments and checking bill status.
