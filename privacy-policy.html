Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how EBBillCalculator.in protects your information.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

At EBBillCalculator.in, we value your privacy above all else. All calculations — including units consumed, sanctioned load, consumer category, state selection, and rooftop solar net metering numbers — take place entirely client-side inside your browser. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data or consumption numbers entered on our site.

2. Browser LocalStorage

We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) exclusively for client-side user preferences. Data stored locally on your device is never uploaded or shared with any remote server.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Cookies

EBBillCalculator.in may utilize standard, privacy-friendly website traffic counters or analytical tools to monitor overall site performance and usage statistics. These tools do not record personally identifiable consumer data.

4. Updates to Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
