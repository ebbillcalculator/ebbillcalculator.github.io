How is Tamil Nadu Electricity Bill Calculated?

Tamil Nadu Generation and Distribution Corporation Limited (TANGEDCO / TNEB) uses a subsidized telescopic tariff structure under Tariff IA (Domestic). For instance, if a household in Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, or Tiruchirappalli consumes 300 units in a month with a 1 kW load:

  • First 100 units @ ₹0.00 = ₹0.00 (100% Free Power Subsidy)
  • Next 100 units (101 to 200) @ ₹4.50 = ₹450.00
  • Remaining 100 units (201 to 300) @ ₹6.00 = ₹600.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,050.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹100 = ₹100.00
  • Electricity Duty (5%): (₹1,050 + ₹100) × 5% = ₹57.50
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,207.50 (Approx ₹1,208)

TNEB Bimonthly Billing System Explained

In Tamil Nadu, residential meter reading is performed once every two months (60-day billing cycle). Under this system:

  • The government provides 200 free units per bimonthly billing cycle (equal to 100 free units each month).
  • If your bimonthly consumption is 400 units, the first 200 units are billed at ₹0, and the remaining 200 units are charged according to the slab rates.
  • This online calculator translates the official bimonthly structure into standard monthly figures so you can budget your monthly household expenses accurately.

Tamil Nadu Electricity Distribution & Payment Channels

All electricity distribution across Chennai, suburban zones, and all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu is administered by:

  • TANGEDCO (Tamil Nadu Generation and Distribution Corporation Ltd): State public utility responsible for end-to-end power supply.
  • Consumers can pay bills online through the official TANGEDCO Web Portal (tnebnet.org), TNEB mobile app, TNeGA e-Sevai centres, or via any BBPS-enabled UPI application using their Consumer Service Number.
