How is Jharkhand Electricity Bill Calculated?

The Jharkhand State Electricity Regulatory Commission (JSERC) sets domestic tariff slabs (DS-II Domestic Urban). For example, if a consumer in Ranchi, Jamshedpur, Dhanbad, or Bokaro consumes 250 units with a 1 kW load (exceeding free unit limit):

  • First 200 units @ ₹4.25 = ₹850.00
  • Remaining 50 units (201 to 250) @ ₹5.50 = ₹275.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,125.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹75 = ₹75.00
  • Electricity Duty (6%): (₹1,125 + ₹75) × 6% = ₹72.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,272.00 (Approx ₹1,272)

Jharkhand 200 Free Units Bijli Yojana

Under the Government of Jharkhand's revised power subsidy welfare scheme:

  • Eligible domestic consumers are entitled to up to 200 units of free electricity every month (increased from earlier 100 units limit).
  • If monthly consumption remains at or below 200 units, the consumer receives a Zero Electricity Bill.
  • If consumption exceeds 200 units, the consumer is charged at standard telescopic rates as per JSERC tariff orders.

Electricity Distribution in Jharkhand

Power distribution across Jharkhand is managed by:

  • JBVNL: Jharkhand Bijli Vitran Nigam Limited supplies all 24 districts across Ranchi, Jamshedpur, Dhanbad, Dumka, and Hazaribagh areas.
  • Tata Steel Utilities (JUSCO): Private distribution licensee supplying Jamshedpur city command area.
