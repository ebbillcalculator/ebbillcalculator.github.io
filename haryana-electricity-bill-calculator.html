How is Haryana Electricity Bill Calculated?

The Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission (HERC) regulates domestic supply (Category I) slab rates. For example, if a household in Gurugram, Faridabad, Panipat, or Karnal consumes 250 units in a month with a 1 kW load:

  • First 50 units @ ₹2.00 = ₹100.00
  • Next 50 units (51 to 100) @ ₹2.50 = ₹125.00
  • Remaining 150 units (101 to 250) @ ₹5.25 = ₹787.50
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,012.50
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹110 = ₹110.00
  • Electricity Duty (10%): (₹1,012.50 + ₹110) × 10% = ₹112.25
  • FSA & Municipal Tax: ₹35.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,269.75 (Approx ₹1,270)

Category I vs Category II Slabs in Haryana

Under HERC regulations:

  • Category I (Up to 100 Units/Month): Low-consumption households enjoy highly subsidized lifeline rates starting at ₹2.00/unit and ₹2.50/unit with lower fixed charges.
  • Category II (Above 100 Units/Month): Households consuming more than 100 units transition to standard slab tariffs (₹5.25 to ₹7.10/unit).
  • Solar net metering under HAREDA offers substantial bill reduction by crediting surplus power generation against your billed consumption.

Haryana Electricity Distribution Companies (DISCOMs)

Power distribution in Haryana is divided between two state corporations under Haryana Vidyut Prasaran Nigam:

  • UHBVN: Uttar Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam Ltd (Panchkula, Ambala, Yamunanagar, Kurukshetra, Kaithal, Karnal, Panipat, Sonipat, Rohtak, Jhajjar)
  • DHBVN: Dakshin Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam Ltd (Hisar, Fatehabad, Sirsa, Jind, Bhiwani, Charkhi Dadri, Gurugram, Faridabad, Palwal, Rewari, Narnaul)
