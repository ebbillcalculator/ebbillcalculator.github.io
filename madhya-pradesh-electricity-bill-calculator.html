How is Madhya Pradesh Electricity Bill Calculated?

The Madhya Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (MPERC) sets residential tariffs (Tariff LV-1) for rural and urban domestic consumers. For example, if a household in Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, or Gwalior consumes 250 units with a 1 kW load (standard tariff):

  • First 50 units @ ₹4.13 = ₹206.50
  • Next 50 units (51 to 100) @ ₹4.68 = ₹234.00
  • Next 100 units (101 to 200) @ ₹6.05 = ₹605.00
  • Remaining 50 units (201 to 250) @ ₹6.45 = ₹322.50
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,368.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹120 = ₹120.00
  • Electricity Duty (9%): (₹1,368 + ₹120) × 9% = ₹133.92
  • Regulatory & FCA Surcharge: ₹30.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,651.92 (Approx ₹1,652)

MP Indira Grah Jyoti Yojana (Subsidy Scheme)

Under the Government of Madhya Pradesh's Indira Grah Jyoti Yojana:

  • Eligible domestic consumers who consume up to 100 units in a month are billed a subsidized flat rate of just ₹100 for the first 100 units.
  • If consumption stays within 150 units, the first 100 units remain at ₹100, and only the next 50 units are charged at regular slab rates.
  • If total monthly consumption exceeds 150 units, the subsidy does not apply and full MPERC tariff rates are charged for all units.

Electricity Distribution Companies in Madhya Pradesh

Power distribution across MP is divided among three regional power companies under MP Power Management Company Limited (MPPMCL):

  • MPPKVVCL (West Discom - Indore): Serves Indore, Ujjain, Dewas, Dhar, Khargone, Ratlam, Mandsaur, Neemuch, Khandwa.
  • MPPKVVCL (East Discom - Jabalpur): Serves Jabalpur, Sagar, Rewa, Satna, Katni, Chhindwara, Damoh, Singrauli.
  • MPMKVVCL (Central Discom - Bhopal): Serves Bhopal, Gwalior, Sehore, Raisen, Vidisha, Hoshangabad, Rajgarh, Guna, Shivpuri, Bhind, Morena.

Madhya Pradesh Electricity Bill FAQs

Frequently asked questions regarding power bills in MP.

You can pay your electricity bill through the MPPKVVCL (mpwz.co.in / mpez.co.in) or MPMKVVCL (mpcz.in) portal, the 'UPAY' mobile app, or via digital UPI payment apps using your 10-digit IVRS Consumer ID.

Under the Indira Griha Jyoti Yojana (IGJY), domestic households consuming up to 100 units per month are billed a nominal concessional rate of ₹100 only.
