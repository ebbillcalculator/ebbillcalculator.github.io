How is Rajasthan Electricity Bill Calculated?

The Rajasthan Electricity Regulatory Commission (RERC) specifies domestic tariff slabs for state discoms. For example, if a household in Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, Udaipur, or Bikaner consumes 250 units with a 1 kW sanctioned load (without state subsidy):

  • First 50 units @ ₹4.75 = ₹237.50
  • Next 100 units (51 to 150) @ ₹6.50 = ₹650.00
  • Remaining 100 units (151 to 250) @ ₹7.35 = ₹735.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,622.50
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹230 = ₹230.00
  • Electricity Duty (15%): (₹1,622.50 + ₹230) × 15% = ₹277.88
  • Urban Cess & Regulatory Charges: ₹25.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹2,155.38 (Approx ₹2,155)

Rajasthan Free Electricity Scheme (Mukhyamantri Nishulk Bijli Yojana)

Domestic consumers in Rajasthan who have registered under the Mukhyamantri Nishulk Bijli Yojana benefit from:

  • Up to 100 Units Free: Zero energy charges and waived fixed charges for domestic households consuming within 100 units per month.
  • Sliding Subsidy for 100 to 200 Units: First 100 units remain completely free, and standard telescopic rates apply only on the units consumed between 101 and 200.
  • Registration Requirement: Beneficiaries must link their Jan Aadhaar Card with their 12-digit K Number at e-Mitra or the Discom portal.

Rajasthan Power Distribution Companies (Vidyut Vitran Nigams)

Power distribution across Rajasthan's 50 districts is managed by three state-owned corporations:

  • JVVNL: Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited (Jaipur, Dausa, Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur, Sawai Madhopur, Tonk, Kota, Bundi, Baran, Jhalawar)
  • AVVNL: Ajmer Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited (Ajmer, Bhilwara, Nagaur, Sikar, Jhunjhunu, Udaipur, Banswara, Chittorgarh, Rajsamand, Dungarpur, Pratapgarh)
  • JdVVNL: Jodhpur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited (Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Bikaner, Barmer, Jalor, Sirohi, Pali, Sri Ganganagar, Hanumangarh, Churu)

Rajasthan Electricity Bill FAQs

Frequently asked questions regarding power bills in Rajasthan.

You can pay your electricity bill through the official Bijli Mitra portal (energy.rajasthan.gov.in), e-Mitra, or through popular payment apps using your 12-digit K Number.

The K Number (Connection Number) is a unique 12-digit identification number printed on the top of your bill, used for online payments, bill downloads, and registering power complaints.
