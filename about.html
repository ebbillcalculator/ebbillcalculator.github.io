About EBBillCalculator.in

Empowering Indian electricity consumers with transparent, accurate, and instant monthly bill calculations.

Our Mission

Understanding power bills in India can often be complicated due to complex state tariff orders, progressive telescopic slabs, fixed meter demand charges, and statutory state electricity duties. At EBBillCalculator.in, our mission is to simplify power bill estimation by providing an easy-to-use, transparent, and accurate online calculator.

What We Do

We maintain an updated tariff database covering major Indian state distribution utilities (DISCOMs) including UPPCL (Uttar Pradesh), MSEDCL (Maharashtra), BSES & TPDDL (Delhi), BESCOM (Karnataka), TANGEDCO (Tamil Nadu), WBSEDCL (West Bengal), PSPCL (Punjab), and more.

Whether you are a residential consumer checking your monthly slab rates, a commercial business estimating demand charges based on Sanctioned Load (kW), or a homeowner calculating monetary savings from Rooftop Solar Net Metering, our platform delivers instant itemized estimates.

Key Features of EBBillCalculator.in

  • State-Specific Tariff Slabs: Accurate telescopic unit slab rates automatically updated for each state.
  • Sanctioned Load Integration: Fixed monthly demand charges calculated precisely per kW load.
  • Consumer Categories: Support for Domestic/Residential, Commercial, Industrial, and Agricultural pump tariffs.
  • Rooftop Solar Net Metering: Direct deduction of solar units (kWh) from total consumption to calculate net energy savings.
  • State Duty & Subsidies: Automatic application of state government taxes, duties, and free unit concessions.
  • Privacy First: 100% client-side calculations inside your browser — zero personal usage data stored or collected.
