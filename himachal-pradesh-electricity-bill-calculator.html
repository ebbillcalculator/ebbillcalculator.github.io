How is Himachal Pradesh Electricity Bill Calculated?

The Himachal Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (HPERC) approves domestic DS slab tariffs. Because Himachal Pradesh generates abundant hydropower, per-unit domestic base rates are among the lowest in India. For example, if a household in Shimla, Dharamshala, Solan, or Mandi consumes 200 units with a 1 kW load (exceeding the 125 free units limit):

  • First 125 units @ ₹1.55 = ₹193.75
  • Remaining 75 units (126 to 200) @ ₹2.95 = ₹221.25
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹415.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹70 = ₹70.00
  • Electricity Duty (4%): (₹415 + ₹70) × 4% = ₹19.40
  • Meter Rent: ₹15.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹519.40 (Approx ₹519)

Himachal Pradesh 125 Free Units Bijli Scheme

Domestic consumers in Himachal Pradesh enjoy the 125 Units Free Power Scheme:

  • All domestic households consuming up to 125 units of electricity per month receive a Zero Bill with zero energy charges, zero fixed demand charges, and waived electricity duties.
  • If monthly consumption exceeds 125 units, the subsidy does not apply to the excess units and billing is calculated on standard HPERC slab rates.

Himachal Pradesh State Electricity Board (HPSEBL)

Generation, transmission, and retail power distribution across all 12 hill districts is managed by:

  • HPSEBL: Himachal Pradesh State Electricity Board Limited. Consumers can make instant bill payments, lodge complaints, and view account statements at hpseb.in or via the HPSEB Mobile App.
