Important information regarding bill estimates, tariffs, and DISCOM variations.

1. General Estimation Disclaimer

The electricity bill calculations provided by EBBillCalculator.in are estimated figures based on published state electricity regulatory commission tariff orders. They are intended solely for informational and estimation purposes and do not constitute an official electricity bill invoice.

2. DISCOM Tariff Variations

Actual electricity bills issued by your distribution company (DISCOM) may differ from our estimate due to variables such as:

  • Seasonal Fuel Supply Adjustment (FSA) or Fuel Surcharge Adjustments.
  • Power Factor surcharges or penalties.
  • Municipal taxes, TOD (Time of Day) metering tariffs, or local cess.
  • Previous billing arrears, delayed payment surcharges, or prompt payment discounts.
  • Specific DISCOM meter rent policies or security deposit adjustments.

3. No Affiliation Notice

EBBillCalculator.in is an independent online utility website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government body, State Electricity Regulatory Commission (SERC), or electricity distribution company (DISCOM).

4. Verification Recommendation

Consumers are strongly advised to refer to their official paper or digital electricity bill issued by their respective DISCOM for exact payment obligations and due dates.
