How is Andhra Pradesh Electricity Bill Calculated?

Andhra Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (APERC) regulates tariffs for domestic consumers (LT-I) categorized into Group A, Group B, and Group C based on annual or monthly consumption levels. For example, if a domestic consumer in Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, or Tirupati consumes 200 units with a 1 kW load:

  • First 30 units @ ₹2.65 = ₹79.50
  • Next 45 units (31 to 75) @ ₹3.35 = ₹150.75
  • Next 50 units (76 to 125) @ ₹5.40 = ₹270.00
  • Remaining 75 units (126 to 200) @ ₹7.10 = ₹532.50
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,032.75
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹95 = ₹95.00
  • Electricity Duty (6%): (₹1,032.75 + ₹95) × 6% = ₹67.67
  • Customer Charges & FPPCA: ₹35.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,230.42 (Approx ₹1,230)

AP Domestic Consumer Groups (Group A, B, and C)

In Andhra Pradesh, consumers are grouped according to consumption thresholds to target state subsidies:

  • Group A: Consumers with annual consumption up to 900 units (≤75 units/month) who receive the highest lifeline subsidies.
  • Group B: Consumers with annual consumption between 901 and 2,700 units (76-225 units/month).
  • Group C: Consumers consuming over 2,700 units annually (>225 units/month) billed under standard commercial/higher residential slabs.

Power Distribution Companies in Andhra Pradesh

Power distribution across AP is served by three state distribution utilities:

  • APEPDCL (Eastern Power): Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, East Godavari, West Godavari.
  • APCPDCL (Central Power): Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam.
  • APSPDCL (Southern Power): Nellore, Chittoor, Kadapa, Anantapur, Kurnool.

Andhra Pradesh Electricity Bill FAQs

Common queries regarding power bills in Andhra Pradesh.

You can pay your electricity bill through the official APSPDCL portal (apspdcl.in) or APEPDCL portal (apeasternpower.com), the Vidyut Seva mobile app, or digital payment apps using your 13-digit Consumer Service Number.
