How is Karnataka Electricity Bill Calculated?

Karnataka Electricity Regulatory Commission (KERC) prescribes a telescopic slab tariff for residential LT-2 consumers. For example, if your home in Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hubballi, or Mangaluru consumes 250 units with a 1 kW sanctioned load (without Gruha Jyothi subsidy):

  • First 100 units @ ₹4.75 = ₹475.00
  • Next 100 units (101 to 200) @ ₹7.00 = ₹700.00
  • Remaining 50 units (201 to 250) @ ₹8.50 = ₹425.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,600.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹110 = ₹110.00
  • Electricity Duty (9%): (₹1,600 + ₹110) × 9% = ₹153.90
  • Regulatory & Fuel Surcharge: ₹30.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,893.90 (Approx ₹1,894)

Karnataka Gruha Jyothi Free Power Scheme

Under the Government of Karnataka's flagship Gruha Jyothi Yojana:

  • Domestic consumers are entitled to up to 200 units of free electricity every month.
  • Eligibility is based on the average consumption of the previous 12 months plus an additional 10% buffer, up to a maximum ceiling of 200 units.
  • If monthly consumption exceeds the entitled subsidized limit but remains below 200 units, the consumer only pays for the differential units. Consumption over 200 units requires paying the full bill amount.
  • Registration must be completed on the Seva Sindhu portal linked with your Aadhaar and Consumer Account ID.

Electricity Distribution Companies (ESCOMs) in Karnataka

Power distribution across Karnataka is handled by 5 government power distribution companies:

  • BESCOM: Bangalore Electricity Supply Company (Bengaluru Urban & Rural, Kolar, Tumakuru, Ramanagara, Chikkaballapura, Davanagere, Chitradurga)
  • MESCOM: Mangalore Electricity Supply Company (Dakshina Kannada, Udupi, Shivamogga, Chikkamagaluru)
  • HESCOM: Hubli Electricity Supply Company (Dharwad, Belagavi, Vijayapura, Bagalkote, Gadag, Haveri, Uttara Kannada)
  • GESCOM: Gulbarga Electricity Supply Company (Kalaburagi, Raichur, Bidar, Yadgir, Ballari, Koppal, Vijayanagara)
  • CHESCOM: Chamundeshwari Electricity Supply Corporation (Mysuru, Mandya, Hassan, Chamarajanagar, Kodagu)
