How is Chhattisgarh Electricity Bill Calculated?

The Chhattisgarh State Electricity Regulatory Commission (CSERC) determines domestic LT-1 tariff slabs. For example, if a consumer in Raipur, Bilaspur, Durg, Bhilai, or Korba consumes 250 units with a 1 kW load (standard tariff):

  • First 100 units @ ₹3.90 = ₹390.00
  • Next 100 units (101 to 200) @ ₹4.10 = ₹410.00
  • Remaining 50 units (201 to 250) @ ₹5.30 = ₹265.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,065.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹80 = ₹80.00
  • Electricity Duty (8%): (₹1,065 + ₹80) × 8% = ₹91.60
  • VCA (Variable Cost Adjustment): ₹25.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,261.60 (Approx ₹1,262)

Chhattisgarh Half Bijli Bill Yojana

Domestic consumers in Chhattisgarh are covered by the popular Half Bijli Bill Scheme (Aadha Bijli Bill Yojana):

  • Under this scheme, domestic households consuming up to 400 units per month receive a 50% flat discount on energy charges directly credited to the monthly bill.
  • No separate application is required; the subsidy is automatically applied to eligible regular bill-paying consumers with clear dues.
  • If monthly consumption exceeds 400 units, the 50% concession does not apply and the bill is charged at full standard tariff rates.

Chhattisgarh State Power Distribution Utility (CSPDCL)

Power distribution across all 33 districts in the state is handled by:

  • CSPDCL: Chhattisgarh State Power Distribution Company Limited. Consumers can track daily meter readings, outages, and make instant bill payments using the official Mor Bijlee mobile app or through cspdcl.co.in using their 10-digit BP Number.

Chhattisgarh Electricity Bill FAQs

Common queries regarding power bills in Chhattisgarh.

You can pay your electricity bill through the official CSPDCL portal (cspdcl.co.in), the 'CSPDCL Mor Bijlee' mobile app, or via digital wallets using your 10-digit BP (Business Partner) Number.

BP Number (Business Partner Number) is a 10-digit unique consumer code displayed at the top right of your CSPDCL bill, required for all online payments and status queries.
