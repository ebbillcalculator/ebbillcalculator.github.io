How is Assam Electricity Bill Calculated?

Assam Electricity Regulatory Commission (AERC) prescribes slab rates for domestic LT consumers. For example, if a consumer in Guwahati, Silchar, Dibrugarh, or Jorhat consumes 200 units in a month with a 1 kW connection:

  • First 120 units @ ₹5.40 = ₹648.00
  • Remaining 80 units (121 to 200) @ ₹6.65 = ₹532.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,180.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹75 = ₹75.00
  • Electricity Duty (5%): (₹1,180 + ₹75) × 5% = ₹62.75
  • FPPPA & Regulatory Surcharges: ₹30.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,347.75 (Approx ₹1,348)

APDCL Smart Prepaid Meter System

APDCL is rapidly installing smart prepaid meters across all urban and semi-urban consumer installations in Assam:

  • Real-Time Tracking: Consumers can check daily usage and remaining balance using the APDCL Smart Meter App or 'myBijli' app.
  • No Disconnection Cushion: Night-time disconnection protection ensures domestic power is not cut off between 6:00 PM and 10:00 AM even if balance reaches zero.
  • Recharge Channels: Instant recharges can be done via UPI, APDCL website, or authorized CSC counters using your 12-digit Consumer Number.

Assam Power Distribution Utility (APDCL)

Entire generation, transmission, and retail power distribution in the state is structured under the Assam State Electricity Board (ASEB) successors:

  • APDCL: Assam Power Distribution Company Limited serves all 35 districts across Lower Assam, Central Assam, and Upper Assam zones.
