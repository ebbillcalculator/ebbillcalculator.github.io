How is Odisha Electricity Bill Calculated?

Odisha Electricity Regulatory Commission (OERC) approves domestic slab rates across all four distribution utilities. For example, if a consumer in Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Rourkela, or Berhampur consumes 250 units with a 1 kW load:

  • First 50 units @ ₹3.00 = ₹150.00
  • Next 150 units (51 to 200) @ ₹4.80 = ₹720.00
  • Remaining 50 units (201 to 250) @ ₹5.80 = ₹290.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹1,160.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹85 = ₹85.00
  • Electricity Duty (5%): (₹1,160 + ₹85) × 5% = ₹62.25
  • Meter Rent & Regulatory Fees: ₹20.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,327.25 (Approx ₹1,327)

Digital Payment Rebates in Odisha

Under OERC guidelines, domestic consumers can benefit from:

  • Digital Payment Rebate: Consumers who pay their electricity bill online via UPI, net banking, or debit card before the due date receive an additional rebate per unit.
  • Timely Payment Discount: Paying within the prompt payment period earns a further reduction on net energy charges.

Tata Power Distribution Companies in Odisha

Electricity distribution in Odisha operates under public-private partnerships managed by Tata Power:

  • TPCODL: TP Central Odisha Distribution Ltd (Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Puri, Paradeep, Dhenkanal)
  • TPWODL: TP Western Odisha Distribution Ltd (Rourkela, Sambalpur, Bargarh, Jharsuguda, Balangir)
  • TPNODL: TP Northern Odisha Distribution Ltd (Balasore, Bhadrak, Baripada, Jajpur, Keonjhar)
  • TPSODL: TP Southern Odisha Distribution Ltd (Berhampur, Ganjam, Koraput, Rayagada, Jeypore)

Odisha Electricity Bill FAQs

Common queries regarding power bills in Odisha.

You can pay your electricity bill through the respective Tata Power DISCOM website (e.g., tpcentralodisha.com), the 'Mo Bidyut' Odisha government portal, or via Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm using your 12-digit Consumer Number.

OERC provides a prompt payment rebate of 10 paise per unit to domestic consumers who pay their electricity bills on or before the due date.
