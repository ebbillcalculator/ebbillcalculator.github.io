How Delhi Electricity Subsidy is Applied

Delhi consumers who have opted into the voluntary power subsidy scheme enjoy:

  • Up to 200 units: Entire energy charges are waived off by the Delhi government, resulting in a zero energy bill.
  • 201 to 400 units: A direct financial subsidy of up to ₹800 is deducted from the bill amount.
  • Above 400 units: Full telescopic slab tariffs apply without any state subsidy.

Delhi Electricity Bill FAQs

Answers to common queries regarding Delhi power bills.

To get a zero bill in Delhi, ensure your monthly consumption stays at or below 200 kWh (units) and make sure you have applied for the Delhi Government Electricity Subsidy through your DISCOM or via WhatsApp/SMS registration.

You can pay your BSES bill through the BSES Delhi website (bsesdelhi.com), BSES Mobile App, Paytm, Google Pay, or through BBPS (Bharat Bill Payment System) using your 9-digit CA Number.

PPAC stands for Power Purchase Adjustment Cost, a regulatory surcharge permitted by DERC to compensate DISCOMs for fluctuating coal and gas prices used in power generation.
