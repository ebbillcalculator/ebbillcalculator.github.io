How is Kerala Electricity Bill Calculated?

Kerala State Electricity Regulatory Commission (KSERC) prescribes a telescopic tariff structure for domestic consumers under LT-1A up to 250 units/month, transitioning to non-telescopic rates beyond 250 units. For example, if a home in Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi, Kozhikode, or Thrissur consumes 200 units with a 1 kW connected load:

  • First 50 units @ ₹3.25 = ₹162.50
  • Next 50 units (51 to 100) @ ₹4.05 = ₹202.50
  • Next 50 units (101 to 150) @ ₹5.10 = ₹255.00
  • Remaining 50 units (151 to 200) @ ₹6.40 = ₹320.00
  • Total Energy Charges: ₹940.00
  • Fixed Demand Charges: 1 kW × ₹90 = ₹90.00
  • Electricity Duty (10%): (₹940 + ₹90) × 10% = ₹103.00
  • Fuel Surcharge & Meter Rent: ₹25.00
  • Estimated Total Monthly Bill: ₹1,158.00 (Approx ₹1,158)

Telescopic vs Non-Telescopic Billing in Kerala

Under KSEB tariff guidelines:

  • Up to 250 units/month: Telescopic billing applies — each slab is charged only for the units that fall within that specific slab bracket.
  • Above 250 units/month: Non-telescopic billing applies — entire units consumed from 0 onwards are charged at higher uniform flat slab rates, which leads to a noticeable jump in monthly bills.
  • Keeping your monthly consumption below 250 units helps you take full advantage of telescopic savings.

Kerala State Electricity Board (KSEB) Overview

Generation, transmission, and retail distribution across Kerala is managed by:

  • KSEB (Kerala State Electricity Board Ltd): The state public utility serving over 1.3 crore consumers.
  • Consumers can pay bills online through the wSS portal (wss.kseb.in), KSEB Quick Pay, KSEB Oruma Net app, or via any BHIM-UPI application using their 13-digit Consumer Number.
